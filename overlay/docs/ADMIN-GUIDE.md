# WestBridge admin panel - staff guide

Sign in at **/admin** (on your PC: http://127.0.0.1:8000/admin).
Five wrong passwords in a row locks that email out for a minute.

## First things to fill in

The dashboard has a **Finish setting up** list. Work through it:

1. **Settings -> Contact details** - the WhatsApp number turns on every
   "Order on WhatsApp" button and the floating WhatsApp icon. Include the
   country code: `+211 9XX XXX XXX`. Phone, email, address and opening hours
   appear in the footer. Anything left blank is simply not shown.
2. **Settings -> Social media** - paste full addresses (`https://facebook.com/...`).
   Only networks with an address get an icon.
3. **Products** - add at least six real items with photos. The homepage shows a
   product strip once four are visible.
4. **Pages -> Privacy Policy / Terms / Warranty** - these show "not published
   yet" until you write them. Have them checked by a lawyer.

## Products

- **Price**: leave blank for "Price on request". To hide every price at once,
  switch off *Settings -> Shop -> Show prices*.
- **Specifications**: one per line, `Label: value`, e.g. `Memory: 16 GB`.
- **Photo**: JPG, PNG or WebP up to 5 MB. Big phone photos are shrunk
  automatically. Square photos on a plain background look best.
- **Show in the shop** off = hidden but kept. The *Visible / Hidden* pill in
  the product list flips it in one click.
- **Featured** items are listed first.

When a customer taps *Order on WhatsApp*, WhatsApp opens with the product
name, SKU, price and link already typed. The sale is agreed in that chat.
The dashboard counts the taps, so you can see which products people ask about.

## Messages

Everything sent through the Contact page lands in **Messages** (the number on
the menu is how many are unread). Opening one marks it read. *Reply by email*
and *Reply on WhatsApp* open your own email or WhatsApp with the customer's
address filled in.

## Projects (portfolio)

Each project is a case study on the Portfolio page. The homepage shows an
"Our Projects" section once at least three are visible. Only write what is
true: no invented clients, figures or quotes. A screenshot (1600 x 1000) makes
a big difference; without one, a drawn panel in the brand colours is shown.

## Pages

Edits the words on About, Services, Contact and the legal pages. Layout and
design do not change. A section left empty is hidden on the site. Under
*Search engines* you can set how each page appears on Google.

## Staff and roles

| Role | Can do |
|---|---|
| Super Admin | Everything, including staff accounts and settings |
| Inventory Manager | Products and categories |
| Content Manager | Pages and projects |
| Sales | Read and answer messages; view products |

Deactivating an account signs that person out on their next click. The last
active Super Admin cannot be demoted or deleted, so nobody can lock the
company out. Forgotten passwords: a Super Admin sets a new one under
*Staff -> Edit*.
